How implied value is computed

TVL × peer multiple × momentum × maturity × breadth

Category median mcap/TVL from tokenized peers, adjusted by 7d momentum, maturity (age with no token) and chain breadth. Fewer than 4 peers uses the global median; multiples bounded to 0.02–2×. Estimates, not token prices.
